      Policy focus scores

      The UNESCO 2005 Convention policy monitoring platform holds 8,155 policy measures reported by 134 countries between 2012 and 2024. Each measure's goal and description are cleaned, stripped of country and place names, and embedded with a multilingual sentence-transformer model (paraphrase-multilingual-MiniLM-L12-v2). The embeddings are compared with short written definitions of six channels through which creative SMEs capture value. A measure's score for a channel is its rescaled cosine similarity to that definition (0–1); a country's score for a report year is the average over its measures.

      Scores measure how closely policy texts match each definition. They do not measure budgets, implementation or reach, and absolute levels differ between channels partly because of how the definitions are worded, so comparisons are most meaningful across countries within a channel.

      Creative firms

      Creative firms are identified in the World Bank Enterprise Surveys with ITC's list of creative-industry ISIC codes (Rev. 4 where recorded, Rev. 3.1 otherwise). Figures use each country's latest survey from 2019 onwards and the survey's sampling weights. The surveys cover registered firms with five or more employees, so micro-enterprises, informal businesses and individual creators are not included. Estimates based on fewer than 30 creative firms are flagged.

      Coverage

      UNESCO reporting depends on national capacity: higher-income countries record more measures, and most reports fall in the quadrennial cycles (2012, 2016, 2020, 2024). Countries without a report in a period are shown in grey.
